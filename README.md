## 👋 About Me

I am an IT Support Officer at a Managed Services Provider in Perth, WA, providing Level 1 and Level 2 support for local government, community organisations and secure, access-controlled client sites. My work covers Microsoft 365, Intune, Autopilot, Entra ID, hybrid identity and client infrastructure, alongside physical security systems such as access control and video surveillance.

I'm working towards systems administration and cloud engineering, and I document everything I build in my home lab as I go.

Outside of work I run [Built for Perth](https://builtforperth.com.au), building websites and providing IT services for tradies and small businesses. Before moving into IT, I worked as an Asphalt Technician across metropolitan and regional sites, and I volunteer as a firefighter with the WA Bush Fire Service.
